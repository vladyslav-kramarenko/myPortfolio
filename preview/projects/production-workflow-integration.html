The problem

Transport and production operations depended on separate sources of information and manually handled trip documentation. Coordinating dispatch, deliveries and accounting required connecting the underlying workflows rather than simply adding another reporting screen.

My role

As part of IT leadership at ZHYTLOBUD-2, I coordinated the work between business users, internal specialists and systems involved in transport requests, concrete production, GPS data and weighbridge records. The internal development team implemented the code; my role focused on technical coordination, requirements and delivery.

Solution approach

Transport orders were planned in 1C; completed trips could then be checked against movement, fuel, delivery and weighing records. The goal was a more connected operating process with fewer disconnected manual steps.

Scope and evidence

The connected workflows operated before February 2022. Because these were internal company systems, source code and production records are not published. I can discuss architecture, implementation trade-offs and cross-team coordination.

For architectural decisions, scope or lessons learned, please contact me directly.
